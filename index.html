About Me

My name is Megha Dhankani. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Home Science from SNDT College, Pune. I have 13 years of experience as an entrepreneur, having started my own confectionery studio, Temptations Forever, in 2002, that specialised in handmade chocolates, baked goods, sugar-free treats and customised hampers for weddings and special occasions.

In 2015, I began my journey as a yoga instructor. Since then, I have continued to build my knowledge through formal training and have taught personal and group yoga sessions. My teaching experience includes working with students one-on-one, conducting group sessions, and teaching at Bloomingdale's School in Bandra West.

I have also conducted group classes at old-age homes, with young children, and for specially abled children and adults. I enjoy making yoga approachable and meaningful for people at different stages of life.
